Why keydrops

Nobody ever replies with the tutorial.

You've seen it a hundred times: someone posts an incredible cover, and the replies are all "tutorial please?" and "how do you do this??" with nothing ever coming of it. keydrops skips the waiting entirely. Paste the link, and it shows you the notes itself.

What's inside

Everything the notes need to make sense.

AI Transcription

Every note, lifted from the recording.

Powered by Basic Pitch, an open piano-transcription model, listening for pitch, timing, and how hard each note was played.

Hand Separation

Left and right, tracked as you play.

Each hand is followed as its own voice through the piece, so a hand crossing into the other's range doesn't get miscolored.

Real Audio Transpose

Shift the key, hear it shift too.

Move a song up or down and the audio actually changes pitch along with the falling notes, not just the visuals.

Sheet Music

Every transcription, also notation.

The same performance rendered as readable sheet music, no separate step or upload required.

Practice Mode

Loop the hard part until it's not.

Mark a section with A/B points and repeat just that passage, at whatever speed you need.

Take It Anywhere

Export to MIDI.

Bring the transcription into any DAW or notation program to keep working on it.

Heads up

Recording live? Still finding its footing.

Uploading a file or pasting a link usually means clean, already-recorded audio. A live mic in your room picks up everything else too, so for now, expect the most accurate results from a file or link, with live recording as the more experimental option while we keep tuning it.
